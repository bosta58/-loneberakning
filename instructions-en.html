Genie – User Guide

Welcome to Genie! This guide explains how to use the app, calculate your pay and communicate with your employer.

Basic information about the app and its purpose.

What is Genie?

Genie is an app designed for employees and employers. It makes it easier to calculate pay, keep track of working hours and improve communication between employees and employers.

What can you do in the app?

  • Calculate pay based on hourly wages.
  • Calculate pay according to a collective agreement.
  • Calculate monthly salary.
  • Track working hours and breaks.
  • Review previous salary calculations.
  • Exchange messages with your employer.

Who can use Genie?

Genie can be used by both employees and employers. Each user signs in with their own account and uses the features available for their selected user role.

Create a user account and access the app.

Registration

The first time you use the app, you need to create a user account. Enter the requested information, your email address and a password.

Selecting a user role

During registration, choose whether you will use Genie as an employee or as an employer.

Sign-in

If you already have an account, enter your email address and password to access the app.

Important

Keep your sign-in details in a safe place and do not share them with other people.

Enter working hours, allowances and tax, and review the calculation.

1. Basic pay calculation

On the Timlön page, you can enter the number of hours worked and the applicable hourly rate. The app calculates your pay based on the information you enter.

2. Work allowances

Depending on the type of work and when you work, you can enter hours in different allowance categories.

3. Tax and holiday pay

Enter the tax percentage and holiday pay percentage in the appropriate fields. Make sure the percentages match your working conditions.

4. Pay period and history

You choose the start date and end date of your pay period yourself, according to your working conditions and your agreement with your employer.

For example, you may choose a period from the 26th of one month to the 25th of the following month. This is only an example – you can choose other dates that suit your own situation.

When a pay period ends, its information is moved to the history. There you can review previous pay periods and their results.

A new pay period starts according to the dates you have selected. This allows you to follow the current period and review previous periods.

5. Reviewing the results

After entering the information, the app displays the calculated pay, allowances, tax and the amount remaining after tax.

6. Saving information

Calculation information can be saved and reviewed later, according to the features available in the app.

7. Checking the calculation

Before relying on the result, check that all entered hours, hourly rates and percentages are correct.

8. Messages and statuses

If you are connected to an employer, you can exchange messages and comments in the app.

  • New comment – you have received a new message or comment.
  • Employee reply – the employee has sent a reply to the employer.
  • Resolved – the employer has marked the message as resolved.

Enter monthly salary and working hours and follow the calculation.

1. Basic monthly salary

On the Månadslön page, enter your monthly salary and working-hour information. The app uses this information to calculate your pay.

2. Working hours and allowances

Enter the hours you have worked and the applicable allowances. Check that all information is correct so that the calculation is as accurate as possible.

3. Pay period and history

You decide the start date and end date of your pay period. Choose dates that suit your work schedule and the way your salary is calculated.

For example, a pay period may run from the 26th of one month to the 25th of the following month. This is only an example – each user chooses dates according to their own situation.

When the period ends, its information is moved to the history. There you can review previous periods and their calculations.

A new period starts according to the dates you have chosen. Information for the new period appears in the current calculation, while previous periods remain available in the history.

4. Tracking working hours

The app allows you to record when you start working, take a break, continue working and finish work.

  • START WORK – records the start of work.
  • BREAK – records the start of a break.
  • CONTINUE – records the return to work after a break.
  • FINISH WORK – records the end of work.

5. Working time

Working time is calculated from the recorded start times, breaks and finish times. Check the information before relying on the final calculation.

6. Tax and allowances

In the appropriate fields, you can review or adjust the tax percentage and other values used in the pay calculation.

7. Reviewing the results

After entering the information, the app displays the monthly salary calculation and the relevant results.

8. Messages and statuses

If you are connected to an employer, you can exchange messages and comments.

  • New comment – you have received a new message or comment.
  • Employee reply – the employee has sent a reply to the employer.
  • Resolved – the employer has marked the message as resolved.
Connect user accounts and communicate between employees and employers.

1. Connecting an employee and an employer

Genie allows employee and employer user accounts to be connected. This makes it easier to exchange information and communicate about work and pay calculations.

2. Connection request

An employer can send a connection request to an employee. The employee can then review the request and decide whether to accept it.

3. Accepting a request

When the employee accepts the request, a connection is established between the employee's account and the employer's account.

4. Employee overview

The employer can view connected employees and use the features available in the employer section of the app.

5. Messages and communication

Employees and employers can exchange messages and comments. This feature can be used for questions, explanations and resolving uncertainties related to work and pay calculations.

6. Message statuses

  • New comment – a new message or comment has arrived.
  • Employee reply – the employee has replied to the employer's message.
  • Resolved – the employer has marked the message as resolved.

7. Important information about connections

Connections are made between the appropriate user accounts. Keep your sign-in details safe and make sure you are connecting to the correct employer.

Send messages, reply and follow message statuses.

1. Sending messages

Employees and employers can exchange messages through Genie. Messages can be used for questions, explanations and agreements related to work and pay calculations.

2. Replying to messages

When you receive a message, you can read its contents and send a reply to the other person.

3. Following conversations

Exchanged messages and replies allow employees and employers to follow their previous communication.

4. Message statuses

  • New comment – a new message or comment has arrived and needs to be reviewed.
  • Employee reply – the employee has sent a reply to the employer.
  • Resolved – the employer has marked the message as resolved.

5. Important

Check your messages regularly so that you do not miss new questions, replies or notifications.

Protect your user account and use the app safely.

1. Protecting your account

Every user is responsible for protecting their sign-in details. Do not share your password with other people.

2. Accuracy of entered information

Check working hours, hourly rates, percentages and other information before relying on the pay calculation result.

3. Reviewing your information

Review your pay calculations and history regularly so that you can keep track of previous and current periods.

4. Using the app on a personal device

If you use Genie on a device that belongs to someone else, make sure you sign out when you have finished.

5. Responsible use

Genie is a tool for pay calculation and working-time tracking. Always check that the information you enter matches your actual working conditions and your agreement with your employer.

Useful advice and a final message to users.

1. Use the app regularly

We recommend that you regularly enter and check your working-time information so that your pay calculations are as accurate as possible.

2. Pay periods

Pay attention to the start and end dates of your pay period. Each user can adjust these dates according to their needs and the way their pay is calculated.

3. History

Completed pay periods are saved in the history, where you can review previous results and compare them with the current calculation.

4. Help and communication

If you have questions about a pay calculation or entered information, you can use the messaging feature to contact your employer, if you are connected.

5. Thank you for using Genie!

The goal of Genie is to make pay calculations and communication simpler, clearer and more transparent for employees and employers.

We wish you success and hope you enjoy using the app!
