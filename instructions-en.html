Genie – User Manual

A simple guide for employees and employers
Welcome to Genie!

This manual will help you understand how Genie works and how to use the app's different features.

Genie is designed to make salary calculations, working time registration and communication between employees and employers easier and clearer.

You can read the chapters in order or open the chapter you need help with.

What Genie is, who the app is for and what you can use it for.

What is Genie?

Genie is a salary and working time app designed for both employees and employers. Its goal is to make salary calculations simpler, clearer and easier to understand.

What can you do with Genie?

The app offers three different salary options:

  • Hourly pay – registration of working hours and hourly salary calculation.
  • Collective agreement – calculation using a basic hourly rate and different supplements.
  • Monthly salary – registration of working time for employees with a fixed monthly salary.

Genie also allows employees and employers to connect their accounts, exchange salary information and send messages to each other.

Our goal

We want to create simple and clear communication between employers and employees, where both parties can follow and check the information.

How to start using the app and choose the correct salary option.

Home page

When you open Genie, you arrive at the app's home page. Here you can select your language and the function that matches your employment.

Choose your language

Genie is available in Swedish, English and Serbian. Choose the language that suits you best.

Choose a salary option

  • T – Hourly pay: for employees who are paid for each hour worked.
  • % – Collective agreement: for employees whose pay is based on a basic rate and contractual supplements.
  • M – Monthly salary: for employees who receive a fixed monthly salary.

Employer

Employers use the employer section to manage company information, employees, salary information and messages.

Select the option that applies to you and follow the instructions on the screen.

Register working hours and view your estimated salary calculation.

Working time calendar

In the Hourly Pay section, you register your working hours in the calendar. You can select a month and year and enter the hours worked for each day.

Register working hours

Working time can be entered in half-hour intervals. Select the correct type of day, for example a normal working day, weekend or public holiday.

Swedish public holidays are displayed in the calendar to help you keep track of days that may involve special compensation.

Overview and saving

The overview shows your registered hours and different categories of working time. You can save your entries and delete a registration if necessary.

The salary period can be adjusted to the period that applies to you. It does not always have to follow a fixed date interval.

Salary information

Enter or check the following information:

  • Hourly rate for normal working hours.
  • Any additional hourly rates or supplements for different working times.
  • Holiday pay as a percentage.
  • Preliminary tax as a percentage.

Salary calculation

Genie calculates gross salary, holiday pay, tax and estimated net salary based on the information you have entered.

The calculation is for information purposes and should be checked against your actual employment conditions and salary information.

Communication with the employer

If you have an active Genie connection with your employer, you can send salary information and receive messages directly in the app.

You can follow the message status and see when the employer has replied or handled the matter.

Register working time and calculate salary with different contractual supplements.

Basic hourly rate

In the Collective Agreement section, enter your basic hourly rate. You can then register working time and different supplements according to your agreement.

Working time and supplements

Use the calendar to register your working hours. Depending on your employment conditions, different supplements may apply, for example:

  • Overtime.
  • Weekend work.
  • Work on public holidays.
  • Unsocial hours and night work supplements.

Salary period

Select the period you want to calculate. Registered hours and supplements are summarised for the selected period.

Holiday pay and tax

Enter holiday pay and preliminary tax according to your information. Always check that the percentages match your employment conditions.

Calculation and communication

Genie summarises registered hours, basic pay and supplements and displays an estimated salary calculation.

With an active connection to your employer, you can send the information and communicate directly through the app's messaging function.

Register working hours, breaks and shifts when you receive a monthly salary.

Monthly salary

This section is intended for employees who receive a fixed monthly salary. You can register working time and follow your shifts during the salary period.

Basic information

Enter your monthly salary and planned working hours. Check that the information matches your employment agreement.

Salary period and night work

Working time is summarised for the current salary period. You can also enter the time interval for night work according to the settings that apply to you.

Register a work shift

The app has the following buttons for working time registration:

  • START WORK – registers when you begin working.
  • PAUSE – registers when you take a break.
  • CONTINUE – registers when you resume working.
  • END WORK – registers when your work shift ends.

Start and end times are saved so that you can follow your work shifts. You can register breaks using the app's available functions.

Overview

The overview shows your registered work shifts and total working time for the period.

Send information to the employer

If you have an active Genie connection, you can send your salary and working time information to your employer.

You can also receive messages and reply directly in the app.

Manage company information, employees, salary details and communication.

Employer section

Employers use the employer section in Genie to manage company information and communication with their employees.

Company information

You can enter company information and add the company logo if the function is available. Save the information when you are finished.

Add an employee

The employer can register information about employees, for example:

  • Name.
  • Address.
  • Personal identity number.
  • Telephone number.
  • Email address.

Name is required. Other information can be left blank if it is not needed.

Manage employees

The employer can view registered information, contact an employee and, when necessary, remove an employee from the list.

Salary information

The employer can view salary information sent by the employee for the different salary options:

  • Hourly pay.
  • Collective agreement.
  • Monthly salary.

Messages

The employer can send messages to employees and receive their replies. Message statuses help both parties follow the communication.

Active connection

To exchange information through Genie, the employee and employer must have an active connection between their accounts. Both parties must be signed in.

How messages work between employees and employers.

Send and receive messages

Genie allows employers and employees to communicate directly through the app.

Messages from the employer

The employer can send a message to an employee. The message appears on the employee's page.

Employee replies

The employee can read the message and send a reply. The reply is then displayed to the employer.

Message status

Messages can have different statuses depending on their stage in the communication process. This makes it easier to see whether a message is new, answered or handled.

Close a matter

The employer can mark a message as handled when the matter has been resolved.

Important information

To use the messaging function, both parties need an internet connection, must be signed in and must have an active Genie connection.

Language settings, signing in and using Genie safely.

Languages

Genie is available in Swedish, English and Serbian. Select your preferred language using the language selector.

Sign in

Genie uses an email address and password for signing in. Employees and employers use their respective accounts.

Password

Your password must contain at least six characters. Never share your password with anyone else.

Employees and employers

When an account is created, the user selects the account role. It is important to sign in using the correct account type.

Connecting accounts

The employer can send a request to connect the accounts. The employee can accept or reject the request. When the connection is active, information and messages can be exchanged.

Data security

Genie uses Firebase for sign-in and data management. Access to information is controlled by security rules and the connection between user accounts.

If something does not work

First check that you have an internet connection and that you are signed in to the correct account.

If the problem continues, try again later or contact the person responsible for Genie.

Important information about salary calculations, data and responsibility.

Informational calculations

Genie's salary calculations are intended as a guide and an estimate. They do not replace an official payslip issued by the employer.

Check your information

The user is responsible for ensuring that the information entered in the app is correct and up to date.

Always check the calculation against your employment agreement, collective agreement and the rules that apply to your employment.

Supplements and compensation

Pay particular attention to overtime, weekend work, public holidays, night work, holiday pay and tax. Rules and percentages may differ between agreements and workplaces.

Employer and employee responsibility

Both the employer and the employee are responsible for checking the information sent and received through Genie.

Technology and internet

Some functions require an internet connection. Temporary interruptions to the internet or external services may affect the app's functions.

Final information

Genie is a practical tool for salary calculations, working time registration and communication. The app does not provide legal or financial advice.

Our goal is to make cooperation between employers and employees simpler, clearer and more transparent.
